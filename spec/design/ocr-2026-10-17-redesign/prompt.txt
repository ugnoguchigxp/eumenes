Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop OCR artifact design proposal for Eumenes, a Japanese productivity application.
Primary request: Design a beautiful, restrained, practical document-reading workspace. Flat front-on screenshot, 1280 x 1000, crisp Japanese typography, generous but efficient spacing. The entire canvas is the artifact; no browser chrome, device frame, marketing decoration, sidebar, or presentation annotations.
Visual language: match an existing soft light shadcn-style interface: cool off-white background hsl(216 16% 92%), almost-white panels, slate text, fine cool-gray dividers, medium blue hsl(214 72% 44%) primary actions, small green completion badge. Precise 8px spacing rhythm, 8-12px corners, subtle shadows only on floating surfaces. Clear professional Japanese sans-serif typography, strong readable hierarchy, no gradients, no glassmorphism.
Composition: a slim 64px artifact header with a small scan icon and the title 「文字を読み取る」 at left. Below is a two-column workspace with 24px outer margins and 20px gap. The left original-media area is about 55% width, the right text-and-actions area about 45%. Both columns align at top, feel like one coherent tool, with balanced whitespace and no tiny buttons or clutter.
Left area: compact toolbar containing outline menu icon button 「資料一覧」, small label 「元資料」, and refresh icon. A large pale-gray image stage below presents a photographed white notice board straight-on, with three clearly readable Japanese lines: 「本日のご案内」, 「受付は午後三時まで」, 「ご利用ありがとうございます」. The board is a realistic photograph with quiet natural lighting and soft blurred indoor background. Thin translucent blue OCR rectangles surround all three lines; the second is visibly selected with a stronger blue border and light blue fill. Image stage is large and visually dominant. Below it a modest filename label 「案内板.jpg」 and a green 「完了」 pill, followed by a subtle hint 「行を選ぶと、元資料の位置を確認できます」. No invented zoom controls or pagination.
Right area: compact input toolbar with upload and camera icon buttons, then subtle one-line support text 「画像・動画 / 16 MiBまで・動画30秒まで」. A refined result section headed 「読み取った原文」 with a small outline copy button 「全文をコピー」. Below it, an unselected plain-text preview of the exact three lines from the photograph with natural line spacing, separated by whitespace and a thin rule rather than another heavy card. Next a small subheading 「行を選択」. Show three simple selectable rows, each with a square checkbox, small muted line number, the same Japanese line text, and a small gray confidence value 0.98, 0.95, 0.93. Only row 02 checked and pale blue, matching the selected image rectangle. A small quiet note 「confidenceは参考値です」. Under the rows, a compact light-blue selection-action area labeled 「1行を選択中」 with two clear outline actions 「コピー」 and 「会話に追加」; this area is distinct from the full-original action. Underneath a single medium blue full-width primary action 「原文を会話に追加」. At the bottom, a separate quiet correction section with a fine top border, small sparkle icon, heading 「補正案」, short explanation 「元画像と原文を照らして、別の案を作成します」 and one outline action 「補正案を作る」. Original and correction remain clearly distinct.
Constraints: polished production UI, accurately rendered Japanese where specified, legible body text, enough contrast, restrained borders, strong alignment. Preserve the existing source/result split, saved-history entry point, file and camera input, multiple line selection, copy and conversation actions, explicit correction creation. No automatic correction, confidence percentages, PDF input, drag-and-drop promise, text editing, export formats, automatic retries, unsupported new features, decorative charts, extra branding, or huge empty margins. All data is illustrative sample content. Produce one finished full-bleed UI mockup image.
